Parametric drawing border and title block for A3 and A1 (`acTpBuildDrawingFrame`): project, structure, sheet title, sheet number, scale, date, stage, design office and signatures, on the `_33_Khungbanve` and `_33_Khungten` layers. Long Vietnamese text wraps or shrinks to stay inside its cell. The bundled template `khung_ban_ve` draws it at any scale. The `ho_so_mo_m1` dossier moves to 0.2.0 with this title block on every sheet; runs saved with 0.1.0 keep their original border when edited or exported.
